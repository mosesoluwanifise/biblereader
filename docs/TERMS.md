# Terms of Use — Scripture Voice

Last updated: 2026-08-15

## 1. The application

Scripture Voice is a Bible reader that generates spoken narration on your own
device. Bible text is public domain (King James Version, World English Bible,
American Standard Version).

## 2. Narration is machine-generated

All narration in this application is **synthesized by an AI speech model**. It
is not a recording of any person reading. No voice you hear belongs to a real
narrator, and no audio is a recording of a human being.
